Polyweb

A small turn-based strategy game for the browser, inspired by the 4X classics. Expand, harvest, train armies and conquer rival tribes.

How to play

How to play

  • Goal: capture every rival city. You lose if you lose all yours.
  • Move a unit: tap it, then a highlighted tile. Attack: tap a red-marked enemy. A unit may move, then attack; attacking ends its turn.
  • Villages 🏘️: walk a unit onto one to turn it into your city. Walk onto an empty enemy city to capture it.
  • Cities give ⭐ stars each turn equal to their level. Select a city (with no unit on it) to train units.
  • Harvest 🍎 fruit or 🦌 animals inside your borders (⭐2) to grow the city's population. Full population levels the city up: more income and bigger borders.
  • Units that skip a turn heal. Cities and forests give a defence bonus. Mountains are impassable. Fog hides what you haven't scouted.
  • Controls: drag to pan, wheel/pinch to zoom, E ends turn, Esc deselects.
