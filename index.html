How to Play

  • Win in Domination by capturing every rival capital and city. In Perfection, have the highest score after 30 turns.
  • Each unit gets one move and one attack per turn. Tap a unit, then a white dot to move or a red marker to attack. Skills change the order: Dash attacks after moving, Escape moves after attacking, Persist attacks again after a kill, a quarter weaker each time.
  • Nothing is taken on arrival. A unit has to start its turn on a village, enemy city or ruin before it can Capture or Examine it, which uses its whole turn. Ruins give stars, tech, an explorer, a veteran or population.
  • Stars arrive every turn: one per city level, plus one for your capital, plus workshops, parks, markets and Diplomacy.
  • Grow cities with harvests and buildings inside their borders: fruit, game, fish, farms, lumber huts, mines and ports. Windmills, sawmills and forges grow with neighbouring farms, lumber huts and mines. Temples add population. A level takes 2, 2, 3, 3, 4… citizens. Each city supports one unit per level, plus one, and offers three rewards to choose from at every level.
  • Technology costs more with every city you own. Climbing opens mountains, Fishing builds ports to board boats, Sailing crosses the ocean and makes Ships, Navigation makes Battleships, Roads halve movement. A fourth ring of ten advanced technologies goes beyond the classic tree.
  • Movement. Forests and mountains stop a unit, and so does stepping next to an enemy. Units board boats only at their own ports.
  • Combat. Damage depends on attack, defence and health. Defenders get ×1.5 in forests (Archery), on mountains (Climbing), in water (Aquatism) and in cities, and ×4 behind city walls. Units that skip moving and attacking can Recover: +4 health in your borders, +2 outside. Three kills unlock Promote: choose +5 health, +1 attack, +1 defence or +1 movement.
  • Roads can be built on any land in your territory, with or without a unit there, and on unclaimed land where your unit stands. When roads join two of your cities, even through other cities, both gain a citizen, once for each pair.
  • Alliances. Open the Game menu and press Ally? beside a tribe you have met. Computer tribes answer at once; human players answer on their turn. Allies can't attack each other or take each other's cities, don't block each other's movement, share vision and each earn +1 ⭐ a turn. If every tribe left is allied, they share the victory. Breaking an alliance makes the other tribe distrust you for 10 turns.
  • Lighthouses stand in the four corners of the world. The first time you sight each one you earn 3 ⭐ and 100 points.
  • Online Play lets friends on different devices share a game turn by turn. Create a game, send the code or invite link, and each player is told when it's their turn. Games are saved on the server, unfinished turns on your device, and Play on another device in the Game menu moves your seat to a phone or another browser.
  • Saving. There are three save slots. Pick one when you start a game; it saves after every turn. Load or delete games under Saved Games.
  • Controls. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. E end turn, N next unit, T tech tree, Esc close or deselect. Shaders can be switched off in the Game menu.
