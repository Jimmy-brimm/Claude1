How to Play

  • Win in Domination by capturing every rival city. In Perfection, have the highest score after 30 turns.
  • Move and attack. Tap a unit, then a white dot to move or a red marker to attack. A unit can move then attack. Attacking ends its turn.
  • Villages and ruins. Walk onto a village to make it your city. Explore ruins for stars, tech, maps or a veteran. Walk into an empty enemy city to capture it.
  • Stars come in every turn from your cities. Spend them on units, technology and city growth.
  • Grow cities by harvesting fruit, animals and fish or building farms, mines, lumber huts and ports inside your borders. The pips under a city's name show its progress to the next level.
  • Tech Tree unlocks units, harvests and movement: Climbing for mountains, Sailing for boats, Navigation for deep ocean.
  • Combat. Damage depends on attack, defence and health. Cities, walls, forests (with Archery) and mountains (with Climbing) protect defenders. Units that skip a turn heal. Three kills make a veteran.
  • Controls. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. E end turn, N next unit, T tech tree, Esc close or deselect.
